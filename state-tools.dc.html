A Blueprint for Future-Ready State Leadership

SEA Change

SEA Change is a functional blueprint for how a high-capacity, future-ready State Education Agency must operate to improve student outcomes.

Developed with current and former state chiefs across varied policy contexts, SEA Change centers on three core commitments:

  1. 1Centering the agency's work on student learning and long-term outcomes
  2. 2Embedding innovation and responsible AI-enabled improvement as expected practice
  3. 3Building the capacity and agency of local leaders, educators, and communities
